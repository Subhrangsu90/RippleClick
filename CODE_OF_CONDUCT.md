# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want RippleClick to be a welcoming project for people with different levels of experience, backgrounds, and perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but own the impact of your words.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Help newcomers participate without gatekeeping.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project community. If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainers through the project's private security or maintainer contact channel.
